Press “Build the belt”, or “Next μ” to go one value at a time.
  1. Fix a value of μ and draw P(x|μ) along the horizontal line through it.
  2. Choose an interval of x holding probability α: the acceptance interval.
  3. Repeat for every μ. The segments form the confidence belt.
  4. Measure x₀ and draw the vertical line through it.
  5. The confidence interval is every μ whose segment the line meets.

Where the 90% comes from: integrating P(x | μ)

Same Gaussian, one fixed μ. The probability that x falls in [x₁, x₂] is the area under the pdf between them. Lower a horizontal line from the top: the x where the curve lies above the line form the window. Stop when its area reaches α. For the Gaussian this gives the central interval, with equal tails.
