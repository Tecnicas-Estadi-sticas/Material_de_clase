Read the interval off the belt

The vertical line at x₀ meets the acceptance intervals of some values of μ. Those μ form the confidence interval, drawn on the μ axis. The coloured strip on top shows which kind of interval each x₀ gives. Drag on the plot or use the slider.

  • Two-sided: the line crosses both edges of the belt, μ₁ ≤ μ ≤ μ₂.

  • Empty: the line misses the belt altogether. No μ ≥ 0 has x₀ in its acceptance interval.
